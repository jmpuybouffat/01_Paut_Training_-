Subject: Byte NDT update — training twins, root inspection twins and the phased-array nozzle roadmap

Hi Shaun,

A short update on Byte NDT: we are moving the training-twin offer towards service introduction, alongside the inspection twins.

The nozzle demonstrator now supports manual single-element scanning, probe orientation around the branch, geometric stops and a first computed scalar backscattered field from a finite flaw surface. It includes English instructions and a repeatable witness test.

Demo: https://byte-ndt-nozzle-formation.streamlit.app/

For root inspections, the direction is a practical inspection twin that follows the inspection workflow and operator actions as closely as possible to work on the machine. The existing C1/C2 applications provide the starting point; the connection to the internal acoustic engines and the relevant hardware workflow must remain explicit.

A phased-array version for nozzle inspections is also planned. The aim is to bring together scan strategy, focal-law/settings preparation and the visual explanation of responses, while keeping the distinction between simulated preparation and instrument acquisition clear.

The current nozzle field is a reduced harmonic scalar model with direct illumination and single scattering. It is not yet a full elastic or wall-bounce solver, and no industrial qualification is claimed.

I would welcome your view on the operator-training priorities and the practical requirements for the next phase.
